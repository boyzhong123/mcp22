✓ Use Chivox if you…
  • ✓Are building an AI tutor, language app, or reading-aloud product
  • ✓Need to know how well someone spoke — not just what they said
  • ✓Want Mandarin assessment that hears tones, erhua, and sandhi
  • ✓Ship through MCP clients (Cursor · Claude · Agents SDK · LangChain)
★ Best fit
✕ You probably want…
  • ✕Whisper / Deepgram / AssemblyAI for plain transcription
  • ✕ElevenLabs / OpenAI TTS for generating speech
  • ✕Azure / AWS for compliance or content moderation
  • ✕Picovoice / Rhino for on-device keyword spotting
Different tool
They stack. Most production teams run Whisper + Chivox together — Whisper transcribes what was said; Chivox scores how well.
